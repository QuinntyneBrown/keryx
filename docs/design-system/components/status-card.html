Overview

The status card answers “is Keryx working?” in one look. Its icon disc, headline and tone change with the pipeline's state; the layout does not. It is a flush card composed of stats, an optional progress bar and the run chart.

  • Four designed states: completed with issues (default), healthy, running, failed.
  • One per page, at the top of Overview.

Anatomy

  1. SummaryA 44 px status disc, an eyebrow, the headline (20 px, 24 px from 768 px) and a meta line with the run id.
  2. StatsThree stats; under the summary with a rule above, beside it with a rule at the left from 1280 px.
  3. HistoryA full-bleed sunken strip with the caption, a link to all runs, and the chart.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1280 px: summary, then stats under a rule, then the history strip.
  • From 1280 px: summary and stats side by side, with a vertical rule and --space-10 between.
  • Padding is 20 px, 24 px from 768 px; the headline grows from 20 to 24 px at 768 px.
  • The chart's fourteen bars flex to the strip's width at every size.

Theming

The disc uses a status background with its icon colour; the strip is --color-bg-surface-sunken. In dark, the sunken strip is darker than the card, keeping the two-level look.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> named by its headline. While running, the meta line is a polite live region that updates no more than every few seconds.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOn “All runs”: goes to Runs.

Focus

Only the All runs link is focusable.

Labelling

The disc's icon is decorative: the headline states the result in words.

Contrast

Headline
Meta
Caption on strip
Warning disc
Success disc
Danger disc
Info disc

Motion

The spinner and the running bar's pulse are the only motion; both stop under reduced motion.

Touch

The All runs link has a 24 px line box.

Content

  • The headline is a sentence fragment that states the outcome with its number: “Completed with 2 failing sources”.
  • The eyebrow says which run: “Last run”, “Current run”.
  • The meta line: when, how long, and the run id in mono.
