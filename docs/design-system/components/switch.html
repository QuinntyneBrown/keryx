Switch

One setting that is either on or off: include Ontario-wide organisations, alert when a whole run fails.

Overview

Use a switch for a single on/off setting. On the Settings page it changes the form, which is then saved with Save changes; it does not act on its own. Several independent options chosen for a submit are checkboxes.

  • The label states the setting; the switch position states its value.
  • Related switches share a <fieldset> and legend (“Also include”, “Also alert”).

Anatomy

  1. Track and thumbA 36 × 20 px pill; the 16 px thumb slides 16 px. Off is --color-border-strong, on is --color-accent.
  2. LabelBody text to the right of the track, --space-3 away.
  3. RowThe <label>; at least --control-height-sm tall and all of it toggles.

Sizes

One size: a 36 × 20 px track in a row at least 32 px tall (44 px on touch).

States

On and off differ by thumb position and by track colour, so the state does not depend on colour.

Both themes

Also include
Also include
  • Disabled while the settings form is saving; the label dims to muted and the track to --opacity-disabled.

Responsive behaviour

  • The label wraps beside the track; the track never shrinks.
  • Rows are 44 px tall on touch devices.
  • Switches stack; they are never placed side by side.

Theming

Three component tokens: --switch-thumb, --switch-track and --switch-track-on. The on colour is the accent, so it is ink in the light theme and volt, with an ink thumb, in the dark theme.

.switch { --switch-track-on: var(--color-accent); --switch-thumb: var(--space-4); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<input type="checkbox" role="switch">, announced as “switch, on/off”. See the WAI-ARIA switch pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
SpaceToggles the switch.
EnterDoes nothing on the switch; it submits the form, as on any input.

Focus

The shared ring around the track, offset 2 px.

Labelling

The wrapping <label> names the setting and does not change with the state (“Ontario-wide organisations”, not “Include / Exclude”).

Motion

The thumb slides and the track recolours over --duration-fast; instant under reduced motion.

Touch

32 px with a fine pointer and 44 px on touch devices: --control-height-sm grows to --target-comfortable under (pointer: coarse). The label is part of the target; dragging is not needed or supported.

Content

  • The label is the thing being turned on, as a noun phrase or a condition: “When a whole run fails”.
  • Avoid negatives: “Include national news”, not “Don't exclude national news”.
  • No “On”/“Off” text beside the switch; the position and the announcement carry it.
