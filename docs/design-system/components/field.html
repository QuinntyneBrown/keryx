Form field

The wrapper every control sits in: a label above, the control, then help or an error underneath.

Overview

Every input, select, range and chip list in Keryx is inside a field, so labels, help and errors are always in the same place. A group of switches, checkboxes or choice cards uses a <fieldset> with a <legend> styled the same as a label.

  • The label is always visible and always above the control. A placeholder is never the label.
  • Help explains the consequence of the value; the error replaces nothing, it is added under the control and linked with aria-describedby.
  • Required is the default and is not marked; an optional field says “(optional)” in its label.

Anatomy

  1. Label--text-label; a <label for>, or a <legend> for a group.
  2. ControlAny input, select, range, chip list or affix group; --space-1-5 below the label.
  3. Help or error--text-caption. Help is muted; the error is --color-danger-fg with a warning icon.

Sizes

One size. The field takes the width of its container; the control inside can be narrowed with <code>.input--short</code> or <code>.input--medium</code>.

States

The field itself has two states; the control inside carries the rest (see text field).

  • Invalid: the error text is added below, the control gets aria-invalid="true", and the value the person typed is kept.
  • Busy: while a form submits, its controls are disabled; labels and help stay readable.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column below 640 px; .field-grid becomes two columns from 640 px.
  • On the Settings page the field column sits beside the section intro from 768 px and under it below.
  • Labels wrap; they are never truncated. Help and error text wrap under the control at its full width.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so both themes and prefers-contrast: more restyle it with no rule of its own. The error text and the invalid edge both come from the danger status tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<label for> tied to the control's id; groups use <fieldset> and <legend>. Help and error are joined to the control with aria-describedby (both ids when both exist).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Click on the labelMoves focus to the control (or toggles a checkbox or switch).

Focus

The field has no focus of its own. On a failed submit, focus goes to the error summary; each summary line links to its control.

Labelling

Every control has a visible label. A chip list, which is not a form control, is labelled by a <p class="field__label"> and the same text in aria-label on the list.

Motion

Nothing animates. The error appears without animation so it cannot be missed by a reader who has motion reduced.

Touch

The label is part of the target for checkboxes, switches and choice cards. Text inputs are 36 px tall, 44 px on touch.

Content

  • Labels are nouns in sentence case with no colon: “Timeout per source”, “Product name”, “E-mail”.
  • Help says what the value does: “Scores from 0.45 to 0.60 wait in Needs review. Below 0.45 are hidden.”
  • An error names the fix and gives an example: “Enter a full URL starting with https://, like https://lifepointfit.ca/events.”
  • Never blame (“Invalid input”) and never clear what the person typed.
