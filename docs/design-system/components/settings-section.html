Settings section (form layout)

The layout of a long form: a titled group with its explanation on the left and its fields on the right.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<section> in a <form>Block: .settings-section

Overview

The Settings page is one form made of sections (Schedule, Relevance, Geography, Storage, Alerts, Account). Each section pairs a heading and one sentence of explanation with a column of fields. Short forms in dialogs do not use it; they stack fields directly.

  • Sections are separated by a hairline, not by cards: the page is one continuous form.
  • The whole page is saved by one save bar.

Anatomy

  1. IntroAn h2 in --text-h3 and a muted hint; a 220 px column (--layout-label-width) from 768 px.
  2. FieldsA column of fields --space-5 apart.
  3. Section--space-6 block padding and a hairline above every section but the first.

Sizes

One size. The form column is capped at <code>--layout-form-max</code>; the intro column is <code>--layout-label-width</code>.

States

The section is layout; its states are those of the form it belongs to.

Form stateWhat the section shows
DefaultSaved values.
DirtyChanged values; the save bar appears.
InvalidField errors under their controls; an error summary above the first section.
SubmittingEvery control disabled; the save bar says “Saving your changes…”.
LoadingSkeleton lines in place of fields.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768 px the intro stacks above the fields with --space-4 between.
  • From 768 px: two columns, 220 px and the rest, --space-10 apart.
  • The form never exceeds 896 px, so lines of help stay readable on wide screens.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so both themes and prefers-contrast: more restyle it with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> inside the page's single <form novalidate>; the heading is an h2 under the page's h1.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterIn a text field, submits the form (the same as Save changes).

Focus

Tab order is the visual order: intro (no stops), then fields top to bottom, section after section, then the save bar.

Labelling

The section heading labels the region. Groups inside use fieldsets with legends.

Content

  • The heading is one noun: Schedule, Relevance, Geography, Storage, Alerts, Account.
  • The hint is one sentence that says what the section governs: “When the collector runs. Times are Toronto time.”
  • Order sections by how the pipeline runs: when, what, where, how long, who is told.
