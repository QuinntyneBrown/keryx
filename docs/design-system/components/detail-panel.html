Detail panel

The article's details beside the table it came from: summary, provenance, why it is held, and the two things staff can do about it.

Overview

Selecting a row on Articles opens the panel. From 1024 px it is a column next to the table, so staff can work down the list; below that it covers the screen. It is for reading and for one-step actions; anything with fields is a dialog.

  • Head: the record's id and Close.
  • Body: status, title, summary, the link to the original, a description list, and an alert when the article is held.
  • Foot: the actions for this status, sharing the row equally.

Anatomy

  1. HeadMono id and Close, on a hairline.
  2. BodyBlocks --space-6 apart; scrolls.
  3. FootOne or two growing buttons above a hairline.

Variants

The foot follows the article's status.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024 px: position: fixed; inset: 0 on the overlay layer, sliding in from the right; it behaves as a modal dialog (focus trapped, Escape closes).
  • From 1024 px: a sticky 400 px column with a left hairline; the table stays usable beside it.
  • Between 1024 and 1535 px the table hides its Category and Region columns and shows them as a second line under the title instead.
  • The foot stays visible; only the body scrolls.

Theming

Surface colour with hairlines, no shadow: beside the table it is part of the page.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <aside aria-labelledby> named by the article title. Below 1024 px, where it covers the page, it is opened as a modal (CDK Dialog) with role="dialog".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter on a row titleOpens the panel and moves focus to it.
EscCloses the panel and returns focus to the row.
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.

Focus

Focus moves to the panel's heading on open and back to the row's title link on close. After Publish or Hide, the panel shows the next state and focus stays on the remaining action.

Labelling

The title is the article's own headline, rendered as text with dir="auto". Close is “Close article details”.

Motion

Slides in 16 px over --duration-base; instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Close is 44 px on touch; the footer buttons span the width.

Content

  • The summary is at most two sentences and is Keryx's own, not a copy of the article.
  • “Open original” always opens the source in a new tab.
  • The alert states the rule that applied: “Held because its relevance score is below the 0.60 publish threshold.”
