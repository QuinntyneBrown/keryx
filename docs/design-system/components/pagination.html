Pagination and table footer

Previous and Next under a table, beside the range of rows being shown.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<nav aria-label="Pagination">Block: .pagination, .table-footer

Overview

Every list the API serves is paged, so every table ends with a footer: which rows are shown, of how many, and two buttons. There are no page numbers; with 18,432 articles, nobody wants page 1,204, they want search.

  • Previous is disabled on the first page and Next on the last.
  • The range sentence is the live region that announces a page change.

Anatomy

  1. Range“Showing 1–10 of 18,432 articles” in --text-caption, subtle.
  2. ButtonsTwo small default buttons with chevrons, --space-2 apart.

  • With one page or less, the buttons are omitted and the sentence reads “Showing all 12 sources”.
  • The page size is fixed per table (10 articles, 12 sources); there is no page-size control.

Responsive behaviour

  • The sentence and the buttons share a row and wrap, sentence first, when narrow.
  • Buttons keep their labels at every width.
  • Small buttons are 44 px tall on touch devices.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so both themes and prefers-contrast: more restyle it with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav aria-label="Pagination"> with two <button> elements. The range sentence has role="status".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Enter / SpaceLoads the previous or next page.

Focus

After a page change, focus stays on the button that was pressed (or moves to the other one if it became disabled), and the table scrolls to its top.

Labelling

“Previous” and “Next” are enough inside a nav labelled “Pagination”. The range is announced politely after each change.

Contrast

Range on page
Button label

Motion

Nothing animates.

Touch

32 px with a fine pointer and 44 px on touch devices: --control-height-sm grows to --target-comfortable under (pointer: coarse).

Content

  • “Showing 1–10 of 18,432 articles”: an en dash in the range, thousands separators, the noun at the end.
  • “Previous” and “Next”, not “Prev”, “Back” or arrows alone.
  • When a filter is active the total is the filtered total.
