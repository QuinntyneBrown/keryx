Empty state and system page

What a screen shows when it has nothing to list, nothing matched, something failed, or the person cannot be here at all.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableCompositeBlock: .empty

Overview

An empty state takes the place of the content that is missing and says three things: what is (not) here, why, and what to do next. The same block, in a centred card on its own, is the whole page for sign in, access denied and not found.

  • First run: nothing exists yet; the action creates the first thing.
  • No results (compact): filters matched nothing; the action clears them.
  • Error (.empty--error): this area failed to load; Try again, plus a reference id.
  • Success (.empty--success): nothing to do is good news.
  • System page: the card alone in a centred page body, its title the page's h1; it may open with the lockup instead of an icon and hold an alert.

Anatomy

  1. Icon disc--size-tile-lg (44 px), subtle; danger or success tint by modifier.
  2. Title--text-h3; a plain statement.
  3. TextOne or two sentences, muted, at most --layout-measure-narrow (44ch) wide.
  4. ActionsOne primary and at most one default button, centred.

States

The empty state is itself a state of its screen. Which one to use:

SituationVariantAction

Nothing to doSuccess, compactNone
Not signed inSystem page, bare shellSign in
Signed in but not allowedSystem page, bare shellSign out / go back

Responsive behaviour

  • Centred at every width; side padding is 20 px, so text wraps comfortably at 360 px.
  • Buttons wrap and stay centred.
  • The system-page card is as wide as its column up to 896 px.

Theming

The disc uses the subtle fill, or the danger or success background with its icon colour. With .empty--card it has the card's surface and edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A heading at the right level (h2 under a page header; the page's h1 on a system page), a paragraph and buttons. An error that replaces content is announced through a role="alert" on the container; a no-results state through the table's polite status.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Enter / SpaceRuns the action.

Focus

When an error state replaces content after an action, focus moves to its heading. On a system page focus starts at the top; the primary action is the first tab stop after the skip link.

Labelling

The icon is decorative; the title says it all. The reference id is selectable text.

Content

  • Title: what is true, plainly. “Nothing has been collected yet”, “No articles match “pickleball””, “The overview didn’t load”.
  • Text: why, then what to do. An error also says what still works: “Collection keeps running on schedule; only this screen is affected.”
  • The primary action is the way forward; the second, if any, is a way elsewhere.
  • A reference id for every server failure: “Reference req_8f2c41d0”.
  • System pages never reveal whether a page exists to someone who may not see it.
