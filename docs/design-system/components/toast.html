Toast

A short message that confirms what just happened, in the corner, without taking focus: a source was added, a run finished, a key was copied.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLive regionBlock: .toast, .toast-region

Overview

A toast reports the result of something the person did, or of the collector they are watching. It appears bottom right, stays at least eight seconds, and leaves on its own, except errors, which stay until dismissed. If the person must act before continuing, use a dialog; if the message belongs to a field or a section, use an alert there.

  • Four tones, by icon colour: info, success, warning, danger. The surface is always the inverse colour.
  • At most one action: Undo, View run, Retry now.
  • At most three are visible, newest at the bottom.

Anatomy

  1. Status icon16 px, in the on-inverse status colour.
  2. Title and textTitle in --text-label; text muted for the inverse surface.
  3. Action (optional)An underlined text button in the on-inverse brand colour.
  4. DismissAn icon button with a 14 px ×.
  5. CountdownA 2 px bar along the bottom showing the time left; absent on errors.

Sizes

One size: 384 px wide (<code>--layout-toast-width</code>) from 640 px, the width of the screen minus 16 px margins below.

States

StateBehaviour
EnteringSlides up 8 px and fades in over --duration-base.
ShowingThe countdown runs for at least 8 seconds.
PausedHovering or focusing anything inside stops the countdown; it restarts on leave.
PersistentDanger toasts have no countdown and wait for Dismiss or their action.
LeavingFades out; focus, if it was inside, moves to the next toast or back to the page.
StackedUp to three, 8 px apart, newest at the bottom; older ones are dropped and remain visible on the Runs screen.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640 px: pinned to the bottom, full width with 16 px margins. Add env(safe-area-inset-bottom) on phones with a home indicator.
  • From 640 px: bottom right, 384 px wide, 24 px from the edges.
  • The region ignores pointer events between toasts, so it never blocks the page; it must not cover the save bar's buttons, so the settings page offsets it by the bar's height.
  • Text wraps; nothing is truncated.

Theming

The toast is the inverse surface: ink in the light theme, paper in the dark theme. Its icons and action use the --color-on-inverse-* tokens, which flip with it: bright status colours and volt on ink, the standard status colours and dark olive on paper. Inside a toast the focus ring is the on-inverse brand colour.

.toast, .toast-region { --color-focus-ring: var(--color-on-inverse-brand); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two live regions that exist from page load: one role="status" (aria-live="polite") for info, success and warning, and one role="alert" for danger. A toast is inserted into the matching region. Toasts do not take focus.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action and Dismiss in DOM order; the region is last in the page.
Enter / SpaceRuns the action or dismisses.
EscDismisses the newest toast when focus is inside the region.

Focus

Focus never moves to a toast by itself. If focus is inside a toast when it leaves, it moves to the next toast or returns to the element that had it before.

Labelling

The title is read first, then the text. Dismiss is labelled “Dismiss”; with several toasts it names its toast (“Dismiss: Source added”).

Motion

Entrance over --duration-base, instant under reduced motion. The countdown is a width change, not a flash. Timing meets WCAG 2.2.1: at least 8 seconds, pausable, and never for errors.

Touch

Dismiss is 32 px (44 px on touch). The action's line box is at least 24 px.

Content

  • Title: what happened, past tense, no full stop: “Source added”, “Key copied”, “Collection failed”.
  • Text: the consequence and the next fact: “LifePoint Fitness Ministry will be checked at 12:00 PM.”
  • Errors say what was not lost: “The API is still serving the previous run.”
  • Action labels are one or two words: Undo, View run, Retry now.
