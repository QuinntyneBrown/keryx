Brand lockup

The Keryx mark and wordmark: a cross whose right arm lifts into a K, the herald raising a call.

Overview

The lockup is the mark (a rounded ink tile with four volt strokes) beside the lowercase wordmark. In the admin it is the home link at the top of the sidebar and the heading of the sign-in card. The brand colours are fixed and do not follow the theme.

  • Mark: a 40 × 40 viewBox, tile rx="10", strokes 4.2 wide with square caps and mitre joins.
  • Wordmark: “keryx”, Geist ExtraBold 800, letter-spacing −0.045em, sized 0.9 × the mark, 10 px from it.
  • Asset files: keryx-mark.svg, keryx-logo.svg (for light backgrounds), keryx-logo-dark.svg (for dark backgrounds) and favicon.ico.

Anatomy

  1. TileA square with a quarter-size corner radius; ink on light, ink 2 on ink, none on volt.
  2. StrokesM15 9V31, M9 16H15, M15 16L28.5 9, M20 20L29 31; volt, or ink on volt.
  3. WordmarkLowercase, ExtraBold, 0.9 × the mark's size.

Four lockups. Pick by background; never recolour to match a theme.

Sizes

The mark is used at 96, 64, 40, 32, 24 and 16 px. The admin sidebar uses 28 px. Below 24 px use the mark alone.

Both themes

  • Both theme panels above show the same colours on purpose: --color-brand-ink, --color-brand-ink-raised and --color-brand-paper are not overridden by any theme.
  • The default .brand (no modifier) is the sidebar form: its tile is --color-shell-raised and its word inherits the text colour.

Responsive behaviour

  • The sidebar lockup is the same size at every width.
  • Never scale the lockup with the viewport; choose one of the six sizes.
  • Clear space around the lockup is at least the tile's corner radius (a quarter of the mark).

Theming

Four component tokens: --brand-mark-size, --brand-tile, --brand-strokes, --brand-word. The lockup modifiers set them from the fixed brand colours.

.brand--on-volt { --brand-tile: transparent; --brand-strokes: var(--color-brand-ink); --brand-word: var(--color-brand-ink); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

As a link: <a class="brand" aria-label="Keryx, go to overview"> with the SVG aria-hidden. As an image: role="img" aria-label="Keryx" on the span, or an <img alt="Keryx">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterAs a link, goes to Overview.

Focus

A volt ring in the sidebar.

Labelling

One name for the whole lockup; the wordmark text is hidden from assistive technology so “Keryx” is not read twice.

Contrast

Volt strokes on the ink tile
Word on ink
Word on white

Motion

Nothing animates.

Touch

As a link its target is the whole lockup: 28 px tall or more with a fine pointer, and at least 44 px tall on touch devices.

Content

  • The name is “Keryx” in running text and “keryx”, lowercase, only in the wordmark.
  • Do not add a tagline to the lockup.
  • Do not translate or re-set the wordmark in another typeface.
