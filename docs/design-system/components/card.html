Card

A bordered white surface that groups one subject: a run's summary, the API's usage, the status of the pipeline.

Overview

A card separates a subject from the grey page. Keryx cards are flat: a hairline edge and a 12 px radius, no shadow. Tables, lists and timelines have the same edge and radius built in, so they are not wrapped in a card.

  • Default: padded (--space-5, --space-6 from 768 px).
  • Flush (.card--flush): no padding and clipped corners, for cards with full-bleed parts such as the status card.
  • Head: a title row with a badge, a meta line, and an action at the far end.

Anatomy

  1. Container--color-bg-surface, hairline --color-border-default, --radius-lg.
  2. HeadTitle row and meta on the left, one action on the right; wraps when narrow.
  3. BodyAny content; here a ruled row of stats.

Sizes

One size; padding grows from 20 px to 24 px at 768 px.

States

Cards are not interactive. A clickable summary is an issue card or a list row.

StateWhat the card shows
DefaultIts content.
LoadingSkeleton lines in the same layout, aria-busy="true" on the region.
Empty / errorAn empty state with .empty--card takes the card's place.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards are fluid; they fill their grid column.
  • Padding is 20 px below 768 px and 24 px from there.
  • The head wraps: the action drops under the title on a phone.

Theming

Light: white on the grey canvas. Dark: --color-bg-surface is one step lighter than the canvas, and the edge is ink-800, so cards read as raised without a shadow.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> with aria-labelledby pointing at its heading, or aria-label when it has none.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable. It is read in document order with the content around it.

Focus

The card takes no focus; controls inside it do.

Labelling

Name the region when it has a heading, so it appears in the landmarks list.

Contrast

Text on surface
Secondary text on surface
Edge (decorative)

Motion

Nothing animates.

Touch

Not interactive, so no target size applies.

Content

  • One subject per card.
  • The title is the thing itself (“Today, 6:00 AM”), not a label for it (“Run details”).
  • At most one action in the head.
