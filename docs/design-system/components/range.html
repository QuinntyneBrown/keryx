Slider

A value on a short scale where feeling the position matters more than typing a number: the relevance threshold.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <input type="range">Block: .range

Overview

Keryx has one slider: the score above which articles publish automatically. Use a slider only when the range is small, the steps are coarse and the exact number is shown beside it. Anything typed precisely is a text field.

  • The current value is always visible in an <output> next to the track.
  • Help under it explains what happens on each side of the threshold.

Anatomy

  1. Track and thumbThe native control, tinted with accent-color; up to 320 px wide and at least --target-min tall.
  2. ValueTabular numbers in --text-label, in a fixed 40 px column so the track does not move as digits change.
  3. Row--space-4 between track and value.

Variants

0.60

Scores from 0.45 to 0.60 wait in Needs review. Below 0.45 are hidden.

The only slider in the product
HTML
<div class="field">
<label class="field__label" for="rv">Publish automatically above</label>
<div class="range">
<input id="rv" type="range" min="0.3" max="0.9" step="0.05" value="0.6" aria-describedby="rv-help">
<output class="range__value" for="rv">0.60</output></div>
<p class="field__help" id="rv-help">Scores from 0.45 to 0.60 wait in Needs review. Below 0.45 are hidden.</p></div>

Sizes

One size. The track is at most 320 px wide and narrows with its container.

  • Changing the slider makes the settings form dirty and shows the save bar; nothing is applied until Save changes.

Responsive behaviour

  • The track fills the row up to 320 px; the value column keeps its width.
  • At 360 px the track is about 240 px, which still gives each 0.05 step a 20 px travel.
  • The thumb's hit area is the browser's; the row is at least 24 px tall.

Theming

accent-color: var(--color-accent) tints the control: ink in the light theme, volt in the dark theme. The unfilled track is drawn by the browser from color-scheme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native range input, which exposes role="slider" with its min, max and value. See the WAI-ARIA slider pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
ArrowRight / ArrowUpRaises the value by one step (0.05).
ArrowLeft / ArrowDownLowers it by one step.
Home / EndMinimum and maximum.
PageUp / PageDownA larger step.

Focus

The browser draws the shared ring around the control.

Labelling

A <label for> from the field. The <output for> shows the value; help is linked with aria-describedby. Dragging is never required: the arrow keys do everything (WCAG 2.5.7).

Contrast

Thumb and fill on page
Value
Focus ring on page

Motion

Nothing animates.

Touch

The row is at least 24 px tall; the thumb can be dragged or the track tapped. Arrow keys are the non-drag alternative.

Content

  • Show the value with the same precision as the step: “0.60”, not “0.6”.
  • The help sentence updates with the value: “Scores from 0.45 to 0.65 wait in Needs review.”
  • The label says what the threshold does: “Publish automatically above”.

<output class="range__value" for="threshold">0.60</output></div>
<p class="field__help" id="threshold-help">Scores from 0.45 to 0.60 wait in Needs review. Below 0.45 are hidden.</p></div>
