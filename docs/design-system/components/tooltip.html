Tooltip

A short label that appears on hover and on focus for a control or mark that has no visible text.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new, not in the mocks yetAPG tooltipBlock: .tooltip, .tooltip-anchor

Overview

The mocks use the browser's title attribute on chart bars and source-type icons. A title does not appear on keyboard focus or on touch, so the system defines a real tooltip for when the product needs one. A tooltip only repeats or names; anything a person must read is on the screen.

  • Text only, one line where possible; never a link or a button inside.
  • Appears above its anchor by default, below with .tooltip--bottom.

Anatomy

  1. Bubble--color-bg-inverse with --color-fg-inverse caption text, --radius-md, --shadow-3, --space-2 from the anchor.
  2. AnchorThe control it describes; wraps both in .tooltip-anchor.

Both themes

  • It shows after 300 ms of hover and at once on keyboard focus; it hides at once on leave, blur or Escape.

Responsive behaviour

  • On touch devices there is no hover: the control's meaning must already be clear from its label or position, and the tooltip is a bonus on long-press only.
  • It flips below its anchor when there is no room above.
  • It never widens the page; it is positioned inside the viewport.

Theming

It is the inverse surface, like a toast: ink in the light theme and paper in the dark theme, so it stands out from whatever it covers.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA tooltip pattern: role="tooltip" with an id, referenced by aria-describedby on the anchor. An icon-only control still needs its own aria-label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the anchor shows the tooltip.
EscHides it without moving focus.

Focus

The tooltip never takes focus.

Labelling

It describes; it does not name. The accessible name comes from the control. Content shown on hover stays while the pointer moves onto it (WCAG 1.4.13).

Contrast

Text
Shortcut

Motion

Fades in over --duration-fast; no movement.

Touch

No target; it is not interactive.

Content

  • Two or three words, no full stop: “Copy key”, “RSS feed”.
  • Never put the only copy of important information in a tooltip.
  • A shortcut is shown after the text in a key cap.
