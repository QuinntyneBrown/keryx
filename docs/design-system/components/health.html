Health

A dot and a short phrase that say how a source is doing: Healthy, Slow, 404 Not Found.

Overview

Health is the status of a source in the Sources table. Unlike a badge it has no pill, so a column of them stays quiet, and its text is the specific condition rather than a category.

  • Four states: healthy (default), warning, danger, paused.
  • The text is tinted in the warning and danger states; a hidden prefix states the category.

Anatomy

  1. Dot--size-dot-lg (8 px), the solid status colour.
  2. TextThe condition; muted when healthy, status-coloured otherwise.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024 px the Health column is hidden and the same element is shown under the source's name.

Theming

Dot colours are the solid status tokens; text colours are the status foregrounds, which pass 4.5:1 on the row, the hovered row and the page.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Text with a decorative dot. A visually hidden prefix (“Failing: ”, “Needs a look: ”) gives the category that the colour gives sighted users.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable. It is read in document order with the content around it.

Focus

Not focusable.

Motion

Content

  • Say the condition in the source's own terms: “404 Not Found”, “Timed out”, “Slow · 12s”.
  • “Healthy” for everything that works; do not vary it.
