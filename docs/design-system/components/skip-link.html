Skip link

The first focusable thing on every page: a link that jumps past the sidebar to the content.

Overview

Keyboard users would otherwise tab through the lockup and six navigation links on every screen. The skip link is invisible until it receives focus, then appears at the top left above everything.

  • It is the first element in <body> on every page, including the bare shell.
  • Its target is <main id="main">.

Anatomy

  1. LinkRaised surface, --radius-md, --shadow-3; parked above the viewport until focused, then --space-4 from the top left.

Shown here in its focused position. On a real page, press Tab once after loading.

Responsive behaviour

  • Identical at every width. Below 1024 px it skips the top bar instead of the sidebar.

Theming

A raised surface with body text; it sits on --z-tooltip, the top layer.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An in-page link (WCAG 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFrom the top of the page: reveals and focuses the link.
EnterMoves focus and the viewport to #main.

Focus

<main> has tabindex="-1" so it can receive focus; the next Tab goes to the first control in the content.

Labelling

“Skip to content”.
