Section head

A heading for a block inside a page, with a link or a count at the other end of the row.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableHeading rowBlock: .section-head

Overview

Use it above a list or a group of cards on a page that has more than one block: “Latest articles — View all 18,432”, “Needs attention — 3 open”. A block that is the whole page needs no section head; the page header is enough.

  • Heading on the left, at most one thing on the right.
  • The right side is a standalone link or a muted count, never a button.

Anatomy

  1. HeadingAn h2 or h3 in --text-h3 (16 px semibold).
  2. Link or countAligned to the heading's baseline at the far end.

Responsive behaviour

  • One row at every width; the heading takes the space the link leaves.
  • At 360 px a long heading wraps; the link stays on the first line's baseline.
  • --space-3 separates the row from the block below.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so both themes and prefers-contrast: more restyle it with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A real heading at the right level for the page outline, and optionally a link. The section it heads uses aria-labelledby pointing at the heading.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring, offset by --focus-ring-offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a pointer click does not show it. It is ink in the light theme and volt in the dark theme, on the sidebar and on toasts.

Labelling

The link's text carries its own meaning (“View all 18,432”), so it does not depend on the heading beside it.

Motion

Nothing animates.

Touch

The link's line box is at least 24 px.

Content

  • Headings are two or three words in sentence case: “Latest articles”, “Needs attention”, “Consumers & keys”.
  • The link says where it goes and how much is there.
  • A count says what is counted: “3 open”.

Class / attributeEffect
.section-headThe row.
.section-head__linkThe link; --sm for the 12 px form used inside the status card.
.text-h3Heading style independent of level.
