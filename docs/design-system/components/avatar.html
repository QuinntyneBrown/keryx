Overview

Keryx shows one avatar: the signed-in staff member in the sidebar footer, beside their name and role. It is initials only; Keryx does not collect photos of people.

  • Two initials, upper case.
  • Decorative when the name is next to it, which it always is today.

Anatomy

  1. Disc--size-avatar (28 px), --color-brand-subtle.
  2. Initials12 px semibold in --color-fg-brand.

Responsive behaviour

Theming

Pale volt with dark olive initials in light; deep olive with pale volt initials in dark. On the ink sidebar both stand out.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <span aria-hidden="true"> beside the name. Alone, it would need role="img" and aria-label with the full name.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable. It is read in document order with the content around it.

Focus

Not focusable. It has no menu: sign-out is a button in Settings.

Labelling

The adjacent name is the label.

Contrast

Initials

Motion

Nothing animates.

Touch

Not interactive, so no target size applies.

Content

  • First letters of the given and family name.
  • The role under the name is the person's Keryx role (“Owner”).
