Tabs

Views of one list by status, each with its count: All, Needs review, Published, Hidden.

Overview

Tabs switch the content of the panel under them without leaving the screen. Keryx uses them once, on Articles. For a filter that combines with others use filter chips or a select; for a two-option toggle, a segmented control.

  • Underline style: the selected tab has an ink underline and medium weight.
  • Each tab ends with a count badge; the count that needs attention is in the warning tone.

Anatomy

  1. Tab listA row with --space-5 gaps on a hairline; scrolls sideways when it overflows.
  2. Selected tab--color-fg-default, medium weight, 2 px underline over the hairline.
  3. CountA count badge; warning tone when the number asks for action.

Sizes

One size: tabs are <code>--control-height-md</code> tall.

States

DefaultHoverFocusSelectedDisabled
Tab

Both themes

  • While the list loads, the tabs stay and their counts are replaced by skeleton pills.
  • A tab with a count of zero stays enabled; its panel shows an empty state. Disabled is styled but unused.

Responsive behaviour

  • The tab list scrolls horizontally when it does not fit (four tabs fit at 360 px with short labels); it never wraps to two rows.
  • The selected tab is scrolled into view on load.
  • Tabs are 44 px tall on touch devices.

Theming

The underline and selected label are --color-fg-default: ink in light, near-white in dark. Tabs do not use the accent fill, which is kept for controls that hold a value.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA tabs pattern with automatic activation: role="tablist", role="tab" with aria-selected and aria-controls, and one role="tabpanel". Build it on the CDK.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the tab list (the selected tab), then on to the panel.
ArrowRight / ArrowLeftSelects the next or previous tab, wrapping.
Home / EndFirst and last tab.

Focus

Roving tabindex: only the selected tab is a tab stop. The ring is drawn around the tab's label and count.

Labelling

The tab list has an aria-label (“Article status”). A tab's name includes its count: “Needs review 6”. The panel is labelled by its tab.

Motion

The label colour changes over --duration-fast; the underline does not slide.

Touch

36 px tall with a fine pointer and 44 px (--target-comfortable) on touch devices, because --control-height-md grows under (pointer: coarse). Tabs are 20 px apart.

Content

  • One or two words; the status as people say it: “Needs review”, not “Pending moderation”.
  • Counts are the real totals with thousands separators.
  • Order: All first, then the tab that needs action, then the rest by size.
