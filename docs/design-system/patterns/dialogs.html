Dialogs and overlays

When something opens above the page, which surface it uses, and how overlays behave together.

Overview

Keryx has five kinds of overlay: dialogs, the detail panel on small screens, menus, toasts and tooltips. Each has one job. An overlay interrupts, so the default is not to use one: show things in the page when you can.

  • Dialogs and menus come from the Angular CDK; nothing is hand-rolled.
  • Only one modal surface is open at a time.
  • Every overlay closes with Escape and returns focus.

When to use

NeedSurfaceModal?
A short form or a decisionDialogYes
Reading one record while keeping the listDetail panelNo from 1024 px; yes below
Two to five actions on a rowMenuNo; closes on outside click
The result of an actionToastNo; never takes focus
The name of an unlabelled controlTooltipNo
A long or multi-step taskA screen, not an overlay—

Structure

Add a topic

Keryx scores items higher when they mention it.

One or two words, like “pickleball” or “youth ministry”.

A form dialog: backdrop, panel, head with Close, body, foot
  • Sizes: 512 px for forms, 416 px for confirmations; both are bottom sheets below 640 px.
  • Stacking: a menu item that opens a dialog closes the menu first. A dialog never opens another dialog; a failed confirmation shows its error inside itself. Toasts sit above dialogs.
  • Dismissal: Close, Cancel, Escape and a click on the backdrop all cancel, except while busy. A dirty form dialog asks before discarding.
  • After: success closes the dialog and shows a toast; the affected row is highlighted or removed.
  • Layers: dropdown 1000, overlay 1200, modal 1300, toast 1500, tooltip 1600; see z-order.

Responsive behaviour

  • Dialogs: bottom sheets below 640 px, centred above; never taller than 92 % of the dynamic viewport; the body scrolls, the foot stays.
  • Detail panel: full screen and modal below 1024 px.
  • Menus stay menus on touch; items grow to 44 px.
  • Toasts span the bottom on phones.

Accessibility

  • Dialogs follow the APG modal dialog pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal, labelled by the title, described by the description; the page behind is inert.
  • Initial focus: the first field; Cancel in a destructive confirmation.
  • Focus is trapped while a modal is open and returns to its trigger on close; if the trigger is gone, to the nearest stable element.
  • Close the dialog by its ref before navigating, or focus is lost.
  • Menus follow the APG menu button pattern; tooltips appear on focus as well as hover and can be dismissed with Escape (WCAG 1.4.13).
  • Toasts are live regions and never steal focus.

Content

  • Dialog titles are the task or a question naming the thing.
  • Confirmations say what is kept and what is lost.
  • The confirm button repeats the title's verb and object.
  • Menu items are verbs; tooltips are two or three words.
