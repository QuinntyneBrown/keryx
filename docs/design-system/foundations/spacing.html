Spacing

A 4 px scale, the rules for space inside and between components, and the fixed sizes of small parts.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and darkSource: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview
  • Scale
  • Fixed sizes
  • Inside and between
  • Tokens

Overview

Space is a 4 px scale from 0 to 96 px, with 2, 6 and 10 px half steps for the insides of small controls. Components carry no outer margin; the layout utilities (stack and cluster) put space between them.

Fixed sizes

Small parts with a fixed dimension have their own tokens, so a dot or a tile is the same size wherever it appears.

Inside and between

WhereSpaceTokens
Inside a pill, badge or tag2 × 8 px, 2 × 6 px--space-0-5 --space-2 --space-1-5
Icon to label4–8 px--space-1 … --space-2
Inside a button or input6 × 12 px--space-1-5 --space-3
Label to control to help6 px--space-1-5
Between fields16–20 px--space-4 --space-5
Table cell12 px; 20 px at the outer edges--space-3 --space-5
Card padding20 px; 24 px from 768 px--space-5 --space-6
Dialog padding20–24 px; 16 px in the foot--space-5 --space-6 --space-4
Between blocks on a page24 px (32 px on API)--space-6 --space-8
Page margin20 px; 32 px from 768 px--layout-margin
Empty-state padding48 px; 32 px compact--space-12 --space-8
  • Inside a component use up to --space-6; between components use --space-6 and up.
  • Space never scales with the viewport width; a breakpoint changes the token instead.
  • A value that is not on the scale is a mistake. The mocks had two (5 px and 9 px); both are now derived from scale values.
