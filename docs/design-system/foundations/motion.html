Motion

Six durations and four easings, what moves and why, and how everything stops under reduced motion.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and darkSource: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easings
  • What moves
  • Reduced motion
  • Tokens

Overview

Motion in Keryx is short and functional: it shows where something came from (a dialog rising, a toast arriving) or that something is still working (a spinner, a shimmer). Nothing moves for decoration, and nothing moves at all for people who ask for reduced motion.

Durations

Press a button to replay. With reduced motion on, the dot jumps.
TokenValueUse
--duration-instant
--duration-fasthover, colour changes, menus
--duration-basepanels, toasts, backdrop
--duration-slowdialogs, sheets
--duration-deliberateskeleton shimmer, indeterminate progress, running pulse
--duration-spinone turn of a spinner

Easings

TokenValueUse
--ease-standard
--ease-enter
--ease-exit
--ease-springreserved: no mock uses it yet
  • standard and enter are the same strong ease-out: fast start, soft landing.
  • exit accelerates away.
  • spring overshoots slightly. No mock uses it; it is reserved.

What moves

ElementMotionDuration
Hover and colour changesColour cross-fadefast
Button pressScale to 0.98fast
SwitchThumb slidesfast
Menu, tooltipScale in from 0.96 / fadefast
ToastSlides up 8 px and fades inbase
Detail panelSlides in 16 px from the rightbase
BackdropFades inbase
DialogScales in from 0.96; a sheet slides upslow
Skeleton shimmer, indeterminate progress, running chart barLoopdeliberate
SpinnerOne turnspin
  • Things enter with motion and leave without it, or faster.
  • Only opacity, translate and scale are animated; layout properties are not.
  • Numbers do not count up and charts do not grow in.

Reduced motion

  • Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the tokens set --duration-fast, -base and -slow to 0 ms, so transitions written with tokens need no media query of their own.
  • components.css also stops every animation, including the loops: the spinner shows as a static ring, skeletons as a flat tint, and the running bar stops pulsing. Text always says what is happening.
  • Nothing flashes. Auto-dismissing toasts last at least 8 seconds and pause on hover and focus.
