Foundation

Shape

Six radii that grow with the element, and two border widths.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and darkSource: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Borders
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Corners are softly rounded and grow with the size of the element: a 2 px chart bar, an 8 px button, a 12 px card, a 16 px dialog. Pills, dots and avatars are fully round. The mark's tile uses a quarter of its size (10 in a 40 unit box).

Radii

TokenValueUse
--radius-xs2px chart bars
--radius-sm4px scope tags, skeleton lines
--radius-md8px buttons, inputs, alerts
--radius-lg12px cards, tables, toasts, menus
--radius-xl16px dialogs, sheets
--radius-fullpills, avatars, dots
RadiusUsed by

sm · 4 pxTags, the sidebar tag, skeleton lines

lg · 12 pxCards, tables, lists, toasts, menus, the callout, the code panel
xl · 16 pxDialogs and bottom sheets
fullBadges, chips, switches, dots, avatars, status discs

Border widths

hairline
thick
TokenValueUse
--border-width-hairline
--border-width-thick
  • Hairline: every edge and divider.
  • Thick: the selected tab's underline, the current navigation bar, the spinner ring, and the invalid input (a hairline edge plus a hairline shadow).

Usage

  • A nested element's radius is the parent's minus the gap between them (a segment inside the segmented track).
  • A full-bleed part inside a card is clipped by the card (.card--flush), not rounded itself.
  • A bottom sheet rounds its top corners only.
