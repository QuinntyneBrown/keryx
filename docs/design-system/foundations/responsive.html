Responsive

Mobile-first rules, what every component does at each breakpoint, touch targets, and how to test.

Overview

Keryx is built mobile first: base styles target a 360 px phone and min-width breakpoints add space, columns and the sidebar. Staff mostly use a laptop, but a failing run is often first seen on a phone, so every screen works at 360 px.

  • No horizontal page scroll at any width down to 320 px.
  • Nothing is hidden on small screens without being available another way.
  • Touch targets are 44 px on touch devices; the same controls are 32–36 px with a mouse.

Breakpoints

Six tiers: base, 640, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1536 px. The full table is on the layout page. The one that matters most is 1024 px, where the sidebar appears and tables show all their columns.

Touch and pointer

  • (pointer: coarse) raises --control-height-sm and --control-height-md to 44 px (--target-comfortable). Buttons, inputs, tabs, chips, menu items and navigation links follow automatically.
  • The smallest target is the chip's remove button at 24 × 24 px (--target-min, the WCAG 2.2 minimum), spaced by the chip around it.
  • Nothing depends on hover. Row hover is a highlight only; tooltips repeat a label that exists elsewhere.
  • No drag-only interaction: the slider works with arrow keys.
  • Inputs under 16 px make iOS zoom on focus; the product sets 16 px on inputs for coarse pointers.
